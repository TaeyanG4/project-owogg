# Reaction Time Test

Reaction Time Test is a free browser game that measures how quickly you respond after the screen turns green. Complete five rounds and receive your average verified reaction time in milliseconds. Lower averages rank higher.

## How to play

1. Start the test and wait while the reaction area is in its ready state.
2. Do not press early. The green signal appears after an unpredictable delay of roughly 2–8 seconds.
3. Click, tap, or press a supported keyboard key as soon as the screen turns green.
4. Repeat for all five rounds.

Pressing before the green signal ends the attempt as a false start, so watching the visual state is more important than trying to predict the delay.

## How scoring works

Each round records the interval between the green cue and your valid response. The final score is the rounded average of all five intervals. OWOGG regenerates the server-approved cue schedule and checks the submitted timing evidence before accepting the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good reaction time?

Results vary with the device, display latency, input method, attention, and environment. Use this game to compare your own attempts under similar conditions rather than as a medical benchmark.

### Can I use a phone or keyboard?

Yes. Mouse, touch, and keyboard input are supported. Compare results from the same input method for a more meaningful trend.

### Why is the wait different each round?

The changing delay discourages prediction and keeps the test focused on reacting to the visible cue.
