# Aim Test

Aim Test is a free browser game that measures how quickly and accurately you can click or tap targets. Clear 20 targets in Normal mode or 30 smaller targets in Hard mode, and try to record the lowest completion time.

## How to play

1. Choose a difficulty and start the test.
2. Click or tap each circular target as soon as it appears.
3. Complete every required hit to receive your verified result.

Normal mode uses larger targets, while Hard mode makes accurate pointer control more demanding. A mouse, trackpad, or touch screen can be used. For a fair personal comparison, use the same device and input method between attempts.

## How scoring works

Your result is the total time in milliseconds from the start of the attempt to the final valid hit. Lower times rank higher. OWOGG generates the target sequence from a server-approved challenge and verifies the ordered hit evidence before accepting the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Aim Test train?

It practices visual attention, pointer control, and quick target selection. It is a casual skill test, not a medical or professional assessment.

### Are the targets in the same place every time?

No. Each attempt receives a challenge that determines a reproducible but changing target sequence.

### Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Touch input is supported, although results from touch and mouse devices are best compared separately.
